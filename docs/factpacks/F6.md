# F6. Select-K heuristics

Date: 2026-10-23. This note records an optional report. No alignment formula changed. `--id-col` stays `analysis_IID`. `--group-col` stays `Dong_Grp`. The barplot K list is the Q files that were passed.

## Flags

`--select-k` is optional and off by default. It requires `--logs`. When it is set, the run writes `select_k.tsv`, `select_k.txt`, `select_k.png`, and `select_k.pdf` from the CV and log-likelihood table that the same run just built. When it is omitted, those files are not written.

`--plateau-fraction` is a float, default `0.05`. That default is an arbitrary qbar setting, not a published constant.

## Table

`select_k.tsv` columns are `k`, `n_runs`, `cv_median`, `cv_min`, `cv_max`, `loglik_mean`, `loglik_sd`, `delta_k`, `min_median_cv`, `elbow_k`, and `plateau_k`. Rows whose `k` is `NA` are ignored. `n_runs` counts the rows at that K, including rows whose CV error or log-likelihood is `NA`. CV statistics use numeric `cv_error` only, and are `NA` when a K has none. `loglik_mean` is the mean of the numeric log-likelihood values. `loglik_sd` is the sample standard deviation, divisor `n - 1`, and is `NA` when a K has fewer than 2 numeric log-likelihood values. The median is the middle value, or the average of the two middle values when the count is even. Floats are `str(float(x))`. Missing values are the string `NA`.

`min_median_cv`, `elbow_k`, and `plateau_k` are repeated on every row.

`min_median_cv` is the K with the smallest `cv_median`. Ties take the smallest K. It is `NA` when no K has a numeric CV error. Min-max CV ranges that overlap a neighbour K are listed as pairs in `select_k.txt`. A neighbour is `K` and `K+1`. That overlap note is qbar's own.

`elbow_k` is the interior K with the largest `d2(K) = cv_median(K-1) - 2 cv_median(K) + cv_median(K+1)`, among K whose `K-1` and `K+1` both have a numeric median. Ties take the smallest K. It is `NA` when no such K exists. This rule is qbar's own. The kneedle method is not implemented.

`plateau_k` is the smallest K at which the gain in median log-likelihood from `K-1` to `K`, divided by the absolute value of the median log-likelihood at `K`, is below `--plateau-fraction` and stays below it for every larger consecutive K. The absolute value in the denominator is qbar's own choice, because log-likelihoods are negative. A larger K with no `K-1` has an undefined gain, so no earlier K is reported. If the series never meets the rule, `plateau_k` is `NA`.

## deltaK

The formulas were read from Evanno, Regnaut, and Goudet 2005, Molecular Ecology 14(8):2611-2620, DOI `10.1111/j.1365-294X.2005.02553.x`, printed page 2614. The open PDF was the University of Lausanne IRIS bitstream `https://iris.unil.ch/bitstreams/c9954cb7-0d89-42cd-8534-4682a1821597/download`. On that page, `L(K)` is the mean likelihood, `L'(K) = L(K) - L(K - 1)`, `|L''(K)| = |L'(K + 1) - L'(K)|`, and `deltaK = m(|L''(K)|) / s[L(K)]`, which expands to `m(|L(K + 1) - 2 L(K) + L(K - 1)|) / s[L(K)]`. The printed minus sign is U+2212. This note uses a hyphen.

qbar sets `L(K)` to the mean of the numeric ADMIXTURE log-likelihood values at that K and `s[L(K)]` to their sample standard deviation. Each K has one mean, so `m(|L''(K)|)` is the absolute second difference of those means. The method was described for STRUCTURE `L(K)`. Using ADMIXTURE log-likelihood is an adaptation. deltaK does not support `K = 1`.

`delta_k` is `NA` at the smallest K and the largest K. It is `NA` where `K-1` or `K+1` is missing, and the text says the difference is undefined across a K gap. It is `NA` for the whole table when any K has fewer than 2 numeric log-likelihood values, and the text says why. If the sample standard deviation at a K is 0, `delta_k` is `NA` at that K and the text says so.

## Text and plots

The first line of `select_k.txt` is: `These are heuristics. None of them identifies a true K. Real populations may not be discrete. Choose K by the biological question and show several K.` The file then states the three rules in plain words, then the deltaK paragraph, then the overlapping neighbour pairs. It does not call any K the preferred choice.

`select_k.png` and `select_k.pdf` are 900 by 900, white, RGB. Three stacked panels are titled `CV error`, `log-likelihood`, and `deltaK`. Each panel draws one point per K, at the median CV, the mean log-likelihood, or deltaK. A panel with no numeric values shows `No data`. Labels are ASCII. The PDF uses the same Pillow save as the bar plot, resolution 150.

## Tests

From the repository root, `python -m unittest test_qbar.py` reported `Ran 112 tests in 3.069s` and `OK`.

The new tests say the numbers are invented. A CV series `1.0`, `0.5`, `0.4`, `0.39` at K 2 through 5 has `min_median_cv` 5. The hand second differences are `d2(3) = 0.4` and `d2(4) = 0.09`, so `elbow_k` is 3. Both summary columns are repeated on every row. A plateau fraction of 0.5 on median log-likelihoods `-100`, `-90`, `-89`, `-89` at K 2 through 5 has gains `10/90`, `1/89`, and `0/89`, and `plateau_k` is 3. One numeric log-likelihood per K makes every `delta_k` `NA`. A table with K 2, 3, 4, and 6 makes `delta_k` `NA` across the gap. A four-K table with two invented log-likelihoods at each K matches the page 2614 expansion computed in the test. A K with sample standard deviation 0 has `delta_k` `NA` and the text says so. The first line of `select_k.txt` is the required sentence, and the text does not contain best K. `--select-k` without `--logs` raises `--select-k requires --logs` in English and `--select-k 需要 --logs` in Chinese. A run without `--select-k` writes no select_k files. The PNG is 900 by 900 and the PDF is non-empty. `--id-col` stays `analysis_IID` and `--group-col` stays `Dong_Grp`.
